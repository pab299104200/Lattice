## Issues encountered

- `store.rs` is 1303 lines, well over the Cadres-coding-standard 800-line ceiling, and at least two functions cross the 50-line ceiling. Recorded as findings F-1 and F-2 in the review.
- `schema.sql` is missing the `memories`-table `scope`, `class`, `verification_status`, `usefulness_score`, and `superseded_by` indexes that `plan.md ## Storage Design` lists. The schema doc `## Index Inventory` heading hands this to T23, which did not add them. Recorded as finding F-3. Phase 4 retrieval should add these before depending on the substrate at scale.
- `MemoryStream::FromStr::Err = &'static str` is inconsistent with the rest of the typed parse errors in this slice. Recorded as F-4.
- `VALUES` slices on every wire-named enum are hand-maintained and can drift when variants are added. Recorded as F-5.
- The migration guide implicitly says legacy data cannot produce `user`/`organization` scoped rows, but doesn't say it explicitly. Recorded as F-6.
- `MemoryStore::write_transition` reuses `MemoryUpdated`/`MemoryInvalidated` for `Stale`/`Contradicted`/`Superseded` transitions. Functionally correct, but Phase 4 retrieval may want a typed transition discriminator. Recorded as F-7.

No blocker- or major-severity findings.
